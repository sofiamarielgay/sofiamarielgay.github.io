My Favourite City: New York City

There's no time like the present to plan a trip to New York City, with its constant energy and endless supply of sights, attractions and activities. Don't wait—a second spent in NYC is just too valuable to miss.

New York City is a premier global destination due to its unmatched density of world-class culture, iconic landmarks, and diverse experiences. Visitors are drawn to the city for its broadway theater, renowned museums like The Met and MoMA, and a culinary scene featuring everything from street food to 72 Michelin-star restaurants.
